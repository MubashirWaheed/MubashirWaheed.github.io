# The Complete Guide to "werden"

"werden" does two kinds of work. **PART A** = what it *means* (its four jobs).
**PART B** = how it *conjugates* across tenses. Read A first, then B.

---

# PART A — The Four Jobs of "werden"

## Job 1: Full Verb — "to become / to get"
Describes a change of state. Takes a **nominative** complement.
Perfekt uses **geworden**.

- Er **wird** Arzt. *(He is becoming a doctor.)*
- Es **wird** kalt. *(It is getting cold.)*
- Sie **ist** müde **geworden**. *(She has become tired.)*

## Job 2: Future — "will" (Futur I)
`werden` (conjugated) + infinitive at the end.
Also expresses present-time probability.

- Ich **werde** morgen **kommen**. *(I will come tomorrow.)*
- Er **wird** wohl zu Hause **sein**. *(He's probably at home.)* — probability

## Job 3: Future Perfect (Futur II)
Action completed by a future point, or an assumption about the past.
`werden` + Partizip II + haben/sein.

- Bis morgen **werde** ich es **beendet haben**. *(By tomorrow I'll have finished it.)*

## Job 4: Passive Voice
**Vorgangspassiv (process):** `werden` + Partizip II. Perfekt uses **worden**.

- Das Haus **wird** gebaut. *(The house is being built.)*
- Der Brief **wurde** geschrieben. *(The letter was written.)*
- Das Haus **ist** gebaut **worden**. *(The house has been built.)* — perfect passive

**Zustandspassiv (state):** uses `sein`, not werden. Describes the finished result.

- Das Haus **ist** gebaut. *(The house is built.)*

### Key distinction to memorize
- Full verb "become" → **geworden**
- Passive "been ...ed" → **worden** (drops the *ge-*)

# PART B — "werden" Across the Tenses

## Präsens (present)
Full verb, or helper for future & passive.

- Es **wird** kalt. *(It's getting cold.)*
- Das Haus **wird** gebaut. *(The house is being built.)*

Forms: werde, **wirst**, **wird**, werden, werdet, werden (watch du/er).

## Präteritum (simple past)
Written/formal past. Same roles, shifted to past.

- Es **wurde** kalt. *(It got cold.)*
- Das Haus **wurde** gebaut. *(The house was built.)*

Forms: wurde, wurdest, wurde, wurden, wurdet, wurden (stem *wurd-*).

## Perfekt (present perfect)
Always with **sein**. Participle depends on meaning:
- "become" (full verb) → **geworden**
- passive → **worden**

- Es **ist** kalt **geworden**. *(It has gotten cold.)*
- Das Haus **ist** gebaut **worden**. *(The house has been built.)*

## Konjunktiv II (subjunctive II)
Präteritum *wurde* + umlaut → **würde**. Hypothetical "would".

- Ohne Heizung **würde** es kalt. *(Without heating it would get cold.)*
- Ich **würde** dir **helfen**. *(I would help you.)*

Rule: **würde + infinitive** = standard "would" for almost all verbs.
Exceptions keep their own form: sein→wäre, haben→hätte, modals→könnte, müsste...

Forms: würde, würdest, würde, würden, würdet, würden.


## Part A answers: "What is werden doing in this sentence?"

werden has four possible jobs. Pick one:

- becoming something (full verb)
- building the future ("will")
- building the future perfect ("will have")
- building the passive ("is being done")

This is about meaning. Which of the four is it?

## Part B answers: "When did it happen?"

Once you've picked a job, you still have to place it in time, past, present, hypothetical. That's tense/conjugation:

- Präsens = now
- Präteritum = past
- Perfekt = past (spoken)
- Konjunktiv II = hypothetical

This is about time, not meaning.

Why they feel redundant: because every sentence needs both at once. Take the passive job (Part A) and run it through the tenses (Part B):

- Das Haus wird gebaut. → passive, present
- Das Haus wurde gebaut. → passive, past
- Das Haus ist gebaut worden. → passive, perfect
