## kommen vs gehen

**Rule:** `kommen` = movement *toward* the speaker, listener, or a reference point. `gehen` = movement *away* from the current location.


- Wir gehen morgen ins Restaurant. — We're going to the restaurant tomorrow. (away from here)
- Wir kommen morgen Abend zu dir. — We're coming to your place tomorrow evening. (toward you)

**Notes:**
- Perspective-dependent: the same trip is `gehen` from where you stand, `kommen` from the destination's point of view.
- `zu dir` (toward a person) pairs naturally with `kommen`.
- `ins Restaurant` = `in` + das (accusative for direction into a place).
