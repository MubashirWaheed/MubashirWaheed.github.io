# LASSEN — Complete Building Block

`lassen` is one of the most flexible and important verbs in German. It is used as:

- Main verb = **to leave / to let (something be)**
- Causative verb = **to have/get something done** (by someone else)
- Permission verb = **to let / to allow**
- Reflexive = **sich lassen** = "can be done" (passive-like)
- Fixed expressions = **lass uns ...**, **lass mich ...**, etc.
- Special Perfekt form = **double infinitive** (not *gelassen* when it has another verb)

The core idea behind almost every use: **you do NOT do the action yourself, you let/cause/allow it to happen.**

---

## 1. Present Tense — Conjugation

`lassen` is irregular: the vowel changes **a → ä** in the du/er-sie-es forms.

| Person | Form |
|---|---|
| ich | **lasse** |
| du | **lässt** |
| er/sie/es | **lässt** |
| wir | **lassen** |
| ihr | **lasst** |
| sie/Sie | **lassen** |

Note: **du lässt** and **er lässt** look the same (both umlaut, both end in -sst / -sst).

---

## 2. `lassen` = to leave (something somewhere)

Here it is a normal main verb meaning **to leave something behind / to leave it as it is**.

> Ich **lasse** die Schlüssel zu Hause.
> = I leave the keys at home.

> Lass das Buch auf dem Tisch.
> = Leave the book on the table.

> Er **lässt** die Tür offen.
> = He leaves the door open.

> **Lass mich in Ruhe!**
> = Leave me alone! / Leave me in peace!

---

## 3. `lassen` = to let / to allow (permission)

**Structure:**

`lassen + Akkusativ (person) + Infinitiv`

The person is allowed to do the action.

> Die Mutter **lässt** das Kind spielen.
> = The mother lets the child play.

> Mein Chef **lässt** mich früher gehen.
> = My boss lets me leave earlier.

> **Lass** mich das machen.
> = Let me do that.

> Sie **lässt** ihn nicht reden.
> = She doesn't let him talk.

---


## 4. `lassen` = to have / get something done (causative)

This is the **most important** use. You don't do the action yourself, you cause someone else to do it.

**Structure:**

`lassen + Objekt + Infinitiv`

> Ich **lasse** mein Auto reparieren.
> = I have my car repaired. (a mechanic does it)

> Er **lässt** sich die Haare schneiden.
> = He has/gets his hair cut. (a barber does it)

> Wir **lassen** ein Haus bauen.
> = We are having a house built.

> Sie **lässt** die Wohnung putzen.
> = She has the apartment cleaned.

### Compare — you do it vs. you have it done

> Ich **repariere** mein Auto.
> = I repair my car myself.

> Ich **lasse** mein Auto reparieren.
> = I have my car repaired (by someone else).

---

## 5. `sich lassen` = "can be done" (passive alternative)

**Structure:**

`sich + lassen + Infinitiv`

This expresses **possibility**, like "can be + past participle." It's a common, elegant alternative to the passive with *können*.

> Das Problem **lässt sich lösen**.
> = The problem can be solved.

> Die Tür **lässt sich** nicht öffnen.
> = The door can't be opened.

> Das **lässt sich** machen.
> = That can be done. / That's doable.

> Der Fehler **lässt sich** leicht finden.
> = The error can be found easily.

### Compare

> Das Problem **kann gelöst werden**.
> = The problem can be solved. (normal passive)

> Das Problem **lässt sich lösen**.
> = The problem can be solved. (same meaning, smoother)

---

## 6. `lassen` = to stop / to give up (doing something)

> **Lass** das!
> = Stop that! / Cut it out!

> Er kann das Rauchen nicht **lassen**.
> = He can't give up smoking.

> **Lass** die Witze.
> = Stop with the jokes.

---

## 7. Past Tense — Präteritum

The Präteritum stem is **ließ**.

| Person | Präteritum |
|---|---|
| ich | **ließ** |
| du | **ließt** |
| er/sie/es | **ließ** |
| wir | **ließen** |
| ihr | **ließt** |
| sie/Sie | **ließen** |

### Examples

> Ich **ließ** mein Auto reparieren.
> = I had my car repaired.

> Sie **ließ** die Kinder spielen.
> = She let the children play.

> Er **ließ** die Tür offen.
> = He left the door open.

---

## 8. Perfekt — Two Different Forms

This is the trickiest part of `lassen`. It has **two** Partizip forms depending on how it's used.

### A. `lassen` alone (as a main verb) → `gelassen`

When `lassen` is the only verb (no second infinitive), the Partizip II is **gelassen**.

> Ich habe die Schlüssel zu Hause **gelassen**.
> = I left the keys at home.

> Er hat mich in Ruhe **gelassen**.
> = He left me alone.

### B. `lassen` + Infinitiv → DOUBLE INFINITIVE (`lassen`, not gelassen)

When `lassen` has a second verb, the Perfekt uses a **double infinitive** at the end: the main verb's infinitive + **lassen** (NOT *gelassen*).

**Structure:**

`haben + ... + Infinitiv + lassen`

> Ich habe mein Auto reparieren **lassen**.
> = I had my car repaired.

> Er hat sich die Haare schneiden **lassen**.
> = He had his hair cut.

> Sie hat mich gehen **lassen**.
> = She let me go.

### Compare the two Perfekt forms

> Ich habe das Buch **gelassen**.
> = I left the book. (lassen alone → gelassen)

> Ich habe das Buch liegen **lassen**.
> = I left the book lying there. (with 2nd verb → double infinitive)

`lassen` always uses **haben** in Perfekt, never *sein*.

---

## 9. Konjunktiv II — `ließe`

The Konjunktiv II is **ließe** (from *ließ* + umlaut ending). In everyday speech people often use **würde ... lassen** instead.

| Person | Konjunktiv II |
|---|---|
| ich | **ließe** |
| du | **ließest** |
| er/sie/es | **ließe** |
| wir | **ließen** |
| ihr | **ließet** |
| sie/Sie | **ließen** |

### Examples

> Ich **ließe** mein Auto reparieren, aber es ist zu teuer.
> = I would have my car repaired, but it's too expensive.

> An deiner Stelle **ließe** ich das.
> = In your place, I would leave that alone.

> Das **ließe sich** machen.
> = That could be done.

Common spoken alternative:

> Ich **würde** mein Auto reparieren **lassen**.
> = I would have my car repaired.

---

## 10. Imperative — Commands with `lassen`

The imperative of `lassen` is extremely common, especially **lass** / **lasst** / **lassen Sie**.

| Person | Imperative |
|---|---|
| du | **Lass!** |
| ihr | **Lasst!** |
| Sie | **Lassen Sie!** |

### "Let's ..." (suggestions)

**Structure:**

`Lass uns / Lasst uns + Infinitiv`

> **Lass uns gehen.**
> = Let's go.

> **Lass uns essen.**
> = Let's eat.

> **Lasst uns anfangen.**
> = Let's begin.

> **Lassen Sie uns beginnen.**
> = Let us begin. (formal)

### "Let me ..."

> **Lass mich** das machen.
> = Let me do that.

> **Lass mich** in Ruhe.
> = Leave me alone.

> **Lass mich** sehen.
> = Let me see.

---

## 11. Future with `lassen`

Future I uses **werden + Infinitiv**. With a causative, both infinitives go to the end.

> Ich **werde** mein Auto reparieren **lassen**.
> = I will have my car repaired.

> Er **wird** sich die Haare schneiden **lassen**.
> = He will get his hair cut.

> Das **wird sich** machen **lassen**.
> = That will be doable.

---

## 12. `lassen` with Reflexive Pronoun (for yourself)

When you have something done **to/for yourself**, add a reflexive pronoun (mir/dir/sich...).

> Ich **lasse mir** die Haare schneiden.
> = I get my hair cut. (for myself)

> Er **lässt sich** ein Haus bauen.
> = He is having a house built (for himself).

> Sie **lässt sich** scheiden.
> = She is getting divorced.

---

## 13. Important Fixed Expressions with `lassen`

> **Lass uns gehen.**
> = Let's go.

> **Lass mich in Ruhe.**
> = Leave me alone.

> **Lass das!**
> = Stop that!

> **Das lässt sich machen.**
> = That's doable.

> **Lass es gut sein.**
> = Let it be. / Drop it.

> **Lass dir Zeit.**
> = Take your time.

> **Lass es mich wissen.**
> = Let me know.

> **Sich etwas gefallen lassen.**
> = To put up with something.

> **Sich nicht unterkriegen lassen.**
> = To not let oneself be beaten down / to stay strong.

---

## 🧠 LASSEN — Master Summary

| Function | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Leave (something) | `lassen + Objekt` | Ich **lasse** die Schlüssel zu Hause. |
| Let / allow | `lassen + Akk. + Inf.` | Sie **lässt** mich gehen. |
| Have done (causative) | `lassen + Objekt + Inf.` | Ich **lasse** mein Auto reparieren. |
| Can be done | `sich lassen + Inf.` | Das **lässt sich** lösen. |
| Stop / give up | `lassen + das` | **Lass** das! |
| Past | `ließ` | Ich **ließ** es reparieren. |
| Perfekt (alone) | `haben + gelassen` | Ich habe es **gelassen**. |
| Perfekt (+ verb) | `haben + Inf. + lassen` | Ich habe es reparieren **lassen**. |
| Konjunktiv II | `ließe` | Ich **ließe** es reparieren. |
| Imperative | `Lass / Lasst / Lassen Sie` | **Lass** uns gehen. |
| "Let's ..." | `Lass uns + Inf.` | **Lass uns** essen. |
| Future | `werden + Inf. + lassen` | Ich **werde** es reparieren **lassen**. |
| Reflexive (for self) | `lassen + mir/sich + Inf.` | Ich **lasse mir** die Haare schneiden. |

## ⭐ The `LASSEN` Family

Memorize these forms together:

```text
lassen
↓
lasse / lässt / lässt / lassen / lasst / lassen
↓
ließ / ließt / ließ / ließen / ließt / ließen
↓
gelassen   (alone)
↓
... + Infinitiv + lassen   (double infinitive with 2nd verb)
↓
ließe / ließest / ließe / ließen / ließet / ließen
↓
wird ... (Inf.) lassen
```

---

## ⚠️ The 3 Most Common Mistakes

1. **Wrong Perfekt**: With a second verb, it's the double infinitive.
   - ❌ Ich habe mein Auto reparieren *gelassen*.
   - ✅ Ich habe mein Auto reparieren **lassen**.

2. **Forgetting the umlaut** in present tense:
   - ❌ du *lasst* / er *lasst*
   - ✅ du **lässt** / er **lässt**

3. **Confusing "let" vs "have done"**: same structure, meaning comes from context.
   - Sie **lässt** ihn gehen. = She lets him go. (permission)
   - Sie **lässt** das Auto reparieren. = She has the car repaired. (causative)
