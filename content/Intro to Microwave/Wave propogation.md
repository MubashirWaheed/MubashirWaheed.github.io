Formula for complex surface impedance 

$$\frac{E_g}{H_g} = Z_F = |Z_F| \cdot e^{\mathrm{j}\Delta\varphi}$$

$$\text{with}\quad Z_F = (1+\mathrm{j})R_\square = \left(1 + \mathrm{j}\sqrt{\frac{\pi f \mu_0}{\sigma_{Cu}}}\right)$$ ,  &nbsp;&nbsp;   $$R_\square$$ is the sheet resistance
