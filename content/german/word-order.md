## Word order of adverbials: TeKaMoLo

> [!info] Rule
> When a sentence has several adverbials, the neutral order is **Te-Ka-Mo-Lo**:
> Temporal (when) → Kausal (why) → Modal (how) → Lokal (where).

| Piece | Meaning | Question |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Te (temporal) | time | wann? |
| Ka (kausal) | reason | warum? |
| Mo (modal) | manner | wie? |
| Lo (lokal) | place | wohin? / wo? |

> [!example] Examples
> - **Wir kommen morgen Abend zu dir.** "We're coming to your place tomorrow evening." (morgen Abend [Te] · zu dir [Lo])
> - **Ich fahre morgen mit dem Auto nach Berlin.** "I'm driving to Berlin tomorrow by car." (morgen [Te] · mit dem Auto [Modal] · nach Berlin [Lo])
> - **Sie geht heute wegen der Prüfung nervös zur Uni.** "She's going to uni today, nervous because of the exam." (heute [Temporal] · wegen der Prüfung [Kausal] · nervös [Modal] · zur Uni [Lokal])

wegen + noun → because of\
weil + sentence → because

> [!tip] Notes
> - Within time itself, go bigger unit to smaller: *morgen* (day) then *Abend* → *morgen Abend*.
> - Neutral order only. Front one element for emphasis and the verb stays in position 2: *Morgen Abend kommen wir zu dir.*


## Verb-second (V2) and inversion

> [!info] Rule
> The conjugated verb is always in **position 2**. Whatever you place in position 1 (subject, time, object, a phrase), the verb follows it, and if position 1 is *not* the subject, the subject moves to right after the verb. This flip is called **inversion**.

> [!example] Examples
> - **Zum Frühstück esse ich immer eine Scheibe Brot.** "For breakfast I always eat a slice of bread." (zum Frühstück [pos.1] · esse [V2] · ich [subj] · immer [freq/Te] · eine Scheibe Brot [obj])
> - **Ich esse zum Frühstück immer eine Scheibe Brot.** Same meaning, subject in position 1, no inversion.
> - **Morgen fahre ich nach Berlin.** "Tomorrow I'm driving to Berlin." (morgen fronted, verb still position 2, subject after)

> [!tip] Notes
> - "Position 2" means the second *slot*, not the second word. A multi-word phrase like *Zum Frühstück* fills one slot.
> - Frequency words (*immer, oft, nie, manchmal*) belong to the time (Te) slot of TeKaMoLo.
> - You front an element (time, object, etc.) to position 1 for emphasis or flow. The verb never moves.

> [!example] Fronted adverbial, verb stays in slot 2
> - **Zum Frühstück esse ich Brot.** "For breakfast, I eat bread."
> - **Am Abend sehe ich einen Film.** "In the evening, I watch a movie."
> - **Nach der Arbeit gehe ich ins Fitnessstudio.** "After work, I go to the gym."
> - **In Deutschland lerne ich Deutsch.** "In Germany, I learn German."
> - **Am Wochenende treffe ich meine Freunde.** "On the weekend, I meet my friends."
>
> Pattern: [fronted phrase] + [verb] + **ich** + [rest]. The verb never lets the subject come before it.
